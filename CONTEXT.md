# Portfolio

The portfolio presents Craig's professional work and includes personal tools.

## Language

**Soccer**:
The tool for retrieving Let's Play Soccer schedules and adding or syncing their
events in Google Calendar.

**Management portal**:
The private tool for viewing and managing Craig's server instances. Its sign-in
is separate from Soccer's Google Calendar authorization.

**Production verification window**:
The observation period used to establish that the selected release works through
the public production site. It is distinct from deployment or approval alone.
