# Soccer schedule

Soccer schedule asks a visitor to choose a calendar output first: an .ics file or Google Calendar. It then reveals the steps that apply: enter team IDs or import temporary Let's Play Soccer access, choose players and teams, review upcoming matches (and past results in Google mode), select games, and download an .ics file or add and sync through Google Calendar.

## Sub-features

- `soccer-entry` offers the calendar output choice first on `/soccer`; Connections and the planner stages appear once an output is chosen.
- `soccer-manual` accepts numeric team IDs and fetches schedules through the LPS boundary. The loopback preview answers from its in-process fake LPS at `/__preview/lps/teams/{id}`; production uses live LPS.
- `soccer-planner-journey` is the choice-first public journey: .ics output, Team IDs, soonest-first rows, deselection memory across refetch, live count and select-all, and the downloaded .ics content. Its output switch (deselection memory across switching to Google and back) runs as the preview's Google account at `/__preview/account/soccer-google`, never as the signed-out visitor.
- `soccer-import` imports a temporary JWT and discovers linked players and teams.
- `soccer-linked-journey` is the granted linked-player journey: .ics output, an import through the dialog, players deciding the teams offered, the shared game once and soonest first, deselection memory across refetch, a team change that scopes the count and memory to the new team set, the downloaded .ics content, and the LPS card recovery when LPS withdraws access.
- `soccer-past-results` reviews scored past games in Google mode, which only a visitor with the soccer grant has, from either schedule source: Team IDs or linked players list every scored past game LPS returned for the chosen teams once, newest first and selected, with no rolling cutoff; games without a score stay out; deselections are remembered for the team set; and the .ics output shows no past results or result Sync. A public Team ID lookup stays upcoming-only.
- `soccer-team-colors` paints each row in its selected team's LPS color, normalized from LPS text into a closed palette, or in a Team ID fallback that no other selected team wears; a game between two selected teams is one row painted half in each color with a neutral home-away score, and names, results, and selection controls stay readable and operable at narrow widths, by keyboard, and in forced colors.
- `soccer-selection` updates selected counts and action availability for upcoming and past groups.
- `soccer-restoration` restores saved player/team workflow on full-page loads and match selection after reload.
- `soccer-ics` downloads only selected upcoming games as `soccer_schedule.ics`.
- `soccer-google` presents disconnected, connected, add, and result-sync states.
- `soccer-result-sync` explains before the explicit Sync that it writes each selected score only into the event this site added to the chosen calendar and never adds a past game, then reports how many results it updated, how many were already current, and how many games it skipped and why.
- `soccer-google-account` shows Google Calendar's separate consent after the Google output is chosen: the disconnected card suggests the site sign-in account and offers another Google account, and the connected card names the account Google reported, offering the site account when it differs.
- `soccer-google-destination` starts a new connection on the primary calendar, lets the visitor save another writable calendar, and pauses Add and result sync with `Calendar selection needed` when the chosen calendar leaves the account or stops accepting writes, until the visitor chooses again.
- `soccer-access` distinguishes public Team ID lookup and ICS download from private LPS import, the team-season history read, and Google Calendar, which need the current `soccer` page grant; the page and every private route make the same decision.
- `soccer-history-notice` shows, where durable Soccer history collection is wired, the import dialog's `Kept indefinitely` notice: an import from that dialog records every linked player's team-season memberships under the site account and enrolls their teams. No environment wires collection yet, so real pages show no notice.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Choose `Soccer` from the shared navigation or footer Tools group.
- On `/soccer`, choose `Download an .ics file` or, where Google is configured, `Google Calendar`.
- Use `Import access` when enabled or enter numeric IDs in `Team IDs` and choose `Fetch schedules`.
- With imported access, choose linked players and confirm teams; then use `Review & output` to select games.
- Choose `Download selected (.ics)` or, when connected, the Google Calendar action.

## Gotchas

- The fake LPS publishes game `7004` for team `479691` after that team's first schedule request, and keeps the count until the server stops. Run `prove-soccer-planner` against a fresh launch; it stops with a relaunch message if `7004` is already present.
- The preview has no site sign-in, LPS key, or Google configuration, so the production `/soccer` entry shows no `Private Soccer access` notice and every private route answers `401`, unless the browser opened `/__preview/account/soccer-linked` or `/__preview/account/soccer-google`. Each entry replaces site Cognito with the preview account and LPS with its own in-process fake for the Soccer page and its LPS routes only, and proves nothing about live Cognito, LPS, or Google. Google Calendar stays off for `soccer-linked`. Each fake publishes `7004` after Pond Mint United's first schedule request, separately from the public fake and from each other, so run `prove-soccer-linked` against a fresh launch too; the steps that use `soccer-google` accept `7004` either unpublished or already published. The `soccer-*` account fixtures supply the grant states instead; they prove rendering around the Cognito boundary, not a live grant decision.
- Durable history collection is wired in no environment, so the preview never collects. `soccer-granted-history` renders the dialog as collection would show it; it does not prove that an import records memberships, which the route tests do.
- The private `GET /soccer/history` JSON read and its `GET /soccer/history/team-seasons` list have no rendered page until #81, and no environment wires the archive they read. The browser proves only the read's signed-out `401`; route tests with fake Cognito, LPS, and DynamoDB in `internal/app/soccer_history_test.go` prove the read's authorization, scored record, and collection context, and those in `internal/app/soccer_history_seasons_test.go` prove the list's current and former seasons, owner isolation, refusals, empty list, and stored-proof list when LPS cannot confirm current teams.
- The LPS card's `Remove retained player data` disclosure appears only for an imported, granted owner with history wired, so only the inert `player-removal` fixture shows it and no preview journey removes anything. `internal/app/player_removal_test.go` drives the rendered removal form through the real routes with fake Cognito, LPS, and DynamoDB: global deletion, kept team and game facts, refusals, the cleared import, and a later recollecting import.
- Retaining an import across a browser restart, withholding it during a site-session timeout, restoring it on the same owner's next sign-in, and clearing it on site sign-out, expiry, or another owner's visit all need site sign-in, which the preview disables. Route tests with fake Cognito and LPS in `internal/app/soccer_import_retention_test.go` prove that behavior; the preview proves only its copy.
- The preview has no Google configuration, so the production `/soccer` entry and `soccer-linked` render the Google output disabled. Google mode comes only from `/__preview/account/soccer-google`, which offers Google output to the granted preview account without a Google connection, or from the `/__preview/soccer/google-*` fixtures. That account's `Connect Google Calendar` and `Use another Google account` controls, and every other `/soccer/google/` route it reaches, answer `Preview only — Google Calendar was not contacted.`; it proves selection handling in Google mode, not Google consent or a Google write.
- No recorded live LPS payload shows a team color yet, so the fake LPS names none for Pond Mint United or Campfire Rovers, and public and linked lookups paint them in Team ID fallbacks: Campfire Rovers purple and Pond Mint United orange. Only Rosehip Athletic, which the fake also serves as `479800`, names a color (`kelly GREEN`, padded and in mixed case). The Google fixtures instead give the example teams the recognized green and orange.
- Fallbacks are assigned per rendered team set, in Team ID order: each colorless selected team starts at the entry its Team ID selects and moves past entries another selected team's LPS color or an earlier fallback took. So selected teams, and the two halves of a shared game, differ while the eight fallbacks last, and the order the Team IDs are entered does not matter. A colorless team can show a different fallback in a different team set (`479691` alone is purple), and two teams that LPS names the same color keep that shared color.
- Every preview fake (public, soccer-linked, soccer-google) returns the same past games: Pond Mint United's `7000` (`4 - 2`, 5 days ago) and Campfire Rovers' `6998` (`2 - 2`, 19 days ago), `6990` (`1 - 3`, 400 days ago), and postponed `6995` without a score. Team ID and linked-player lookups of the same teams share the selection key `479147-479691`, so clear selection storage between sources when a proof needs fresh defaults.

- Never paste or record a real JWT in proof artifacts.
- The fixture buttons that would mutate LPS or Google are intentionally disabled, and the `soccer-google` account's Google controls answer `Preview only`. That is the expected safety behavior, not a live integration result.
- Preview cannot prove production Google add/result-sync enabling; record that as an external harness boundary. The result Sync fixtures render its reports only; no preview path sends a Sync.
- An ICS response is a real local file side effect even in preview. Preserve it as evidence and inspect its contents; a download toast alone is insufficient.
- Selection state uses browser `sessionStorage` keyed by the loaded team fingerprint. Use the run-scoped browser session and clear it when comparing unrelated fixtures.
- Live Google OAuth requires user-driven consent and configured external state. Do not claim it from local route behavior.
